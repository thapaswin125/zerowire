Key Points Introduction ARTEX is a Chinese open-source, AI-powered penetration testing platform designed to help security teams assess systems they own or are authorized to test. It uses large language models (LLMs) to coordinate multiple AI agents that plan tests, run security tools, and record evidence of potential vulnerabilities.
