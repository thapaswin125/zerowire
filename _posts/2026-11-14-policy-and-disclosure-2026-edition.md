Since 2021, Project Zero has followed a “90 + 30” vulnerability disclosure policy, allowing vendors up to 90 days to remediate a vulnerability plus an extra 30 days “soak time” for patch rollout. Our goal was to encourage faster patching and give vendors time to deliver patches to users.
