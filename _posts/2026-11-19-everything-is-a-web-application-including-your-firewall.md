One of the early targets of PHP2Shell exploitation was the firewall. On October 28, Palo Alto Networks confirmed that attackers were exploiting the two PHP vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, against PAN-OS management interfaces exposed to the internet. Both flaws let anyone execute code on the web server without authentication.
