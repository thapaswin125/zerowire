Ransomware reached record levels in Q3 2026, but the scale of activity is only part of what changed. The ecosystem producing those attacks is becoming increasingly fragmented: more ransomware brands are claiming victims, the top groups account for a smaller share of overall activity, and several major organizations are pushing back against high-value ransom demands.
