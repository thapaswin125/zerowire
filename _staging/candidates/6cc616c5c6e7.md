Meta-owned WhatsApp has addressed security vulnerabilities, including one impacting its Android app that it suspects may have been exploited in connection with scam messages. The vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-23871 (CVSS score: 3.5), has been described as a case of incomplete validation of external ad-reply URLs in WhatsApp for Android and WhatsApp Business for Android before 2.26.17.4.
