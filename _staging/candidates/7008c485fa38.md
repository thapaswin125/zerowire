A critical flaw in VMware vCenter lets an attacker with network access bypass authentication and gain unauthorized access to the system, Broadcom, which owns VMware, said in an advisory published today. Fixed versions are out for all supported branches, and the company lists no workaround. vCenter is the central console for managing an organization's ESX hosts and virtual machines.
