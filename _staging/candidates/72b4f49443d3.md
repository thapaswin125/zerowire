CISA, the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre), and partners are releasing joint guidance, Managing AI Token Consumption for Operational Resilience , to help organizations understand and manage token usage in support of operations. As organizations adopt third-party super intelligence (SI) services, token-based usage models can affect cost, performance, and access to critical services.
