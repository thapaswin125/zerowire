CVE ID : CVE-2026-18480 Published : Sept. 6, 2026, 7:16 a.m. | 1 hour, 28 minutes ago Description : The SureCart WordPress plugin before 4.6.3 does not ensure that the account affected by a customer update is the same account its permission check authorised, allowing users with a subscriber-level account to change another user's email address, including an administrator's, and take over that account via a password reset.
