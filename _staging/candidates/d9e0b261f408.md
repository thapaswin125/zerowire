Security leaders are about to hand AI agents a lot more authority. In a recent a16z survey of CISOs at organizations with more than 1,000 employees, 36% said they are comfortable today letting AI agents act autonomously on low-risk security actions. In 12 months from now, 60% expect to be comfortable at that level.
