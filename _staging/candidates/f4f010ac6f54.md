CVE ID : CVE-2026-92403 Published : Sept. 19, 2026, 6 a.m. | 56 minutes ago Description : The Secure Custom Fields WordPress plugin before 6.9.4 does not properly verify that a front-end form submission corresponds to the form that was rendered to the visitor, allowing unauthenticated users to submit against a different registered form and modify the title and content of the post that form is bound to.
